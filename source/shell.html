CULTURE IS NOT A GLOBAL UNLOCK

What they have learned.

Experiments consume materials. Physical feedback is recorded. Jev then decides what the individual infers. A discovery belongs to that learner until another person independently learns it.

These are the engine's 13 implemented abilities, not an unlimited invention system or an archaeological reconstruction. Practice requirements and physical outcomes are explicit simulation rules. There is no timed unlock, forced technological sequence, global knowledge injection or automatic research policy.

SIGNAL ≠ SHARED MEANING

Before anyone understands.

The sender selects a referent, a vocal token and a gesture through Jev. The receiver gets the observable token and gesture, not the sender's private intent. Their interpretation is another Jev decision. Misunderstanding is retained.

THE QUESTION, NOT A PRETEND ANSWER

What would count as evidence?

Watch whether a subject distinguishes its own actions, retains consequences, develops models of other bodies and shares meaning. These are inspectable behaviours, not a consciousness meter.

No percentage here means "conscious". This experiment does not establish subjective experience, sentience or human intelligence. Jev is already pretrained; the subjects' empty initial memories do not erase the model's knowledge. Learning here changes persistent world state and future context, not the model's weights.

Keep the evidence falsifiable.

A failed interpretation remains a failure. An experiment without repeatable support grants no ability. A receiver can decline. A confidence value is the model's returned statistic, not a probability that the character is conscious. A successful memory probe must match an actual recorded episode.

THE EXPERIMENT CONTRACT

Real decisions. Simulated lives.

ORIGIN is a browser life-and-learning experiment. The network requests, returned choices, finite inventories, world changes and receipts are functional. The people and environment are fictional. The 3D humans are original articulated procedural models, not filmed people or photorealistic scans.

Exactly what requires Jev?

Every voluntary action, goal, signal, interpretation, acceptance, refusal, animal reaction and learned belief needs a Jev response. Optional planner proposals are labelled separately and cannot modify the world. Invalid required answers reject the uncommitted turn. No fallback policy or automatic retry is used.

What does JavaScript do?

It enforces explicit world rules: feasibility, finite inventory transfers, practice prerequisites, physical outcomes, body costs, plants, fire fuel, movement display and logging. These are mechanics, not decisions by an agent. The five-minute world clock advances once per committed turn. The next scheduled subject sees the result before choosing. A fair round-robin scheduler decides whose turn it is, not what they do.

What does "starting with nothing" mean?

Each adult subject starts with an empty inventory, no learned ability, no vocabulary and no social relationships. Bodies have initial needs and basic motor affordances. Their visible clothing and bodies are initial conditions, not crafted inventions. Jev is pretrained, not a newly born brain. The engine limits actions using individual experience; it cannot prove that pretrained knowledge has no influence.

Learning and technological limits

Ordinary fruit consumption records measured before/after satiety. After two positive personal episodes, Jev must decide whether a limited causal belief is supported; no ability is granted merely for eating. Imported v4 episodes are not retroactively turned into abilities. Thirteen bounded abilities are implemented, including food recognition, tools, fire, shelter, hunting, signals, cultivation, ceramics, metalworking, external memory and simple mechanisms. Material costs and practice thresholds are designed abstractions, not a scientific physics or human-development model. New geometry and arbitrary inventions are not generated. No one is guaranteed to discover everything or remain alive indefinitely. The updated prompts and feedback have not been validated against a fresh live Jev run in the authoring environment.

Stopping, persistence and spending

Pause cancels requests with a named reason. Already committed turns remain valid. A provider may charge for an aborted call. Hiding the tab pauses execution. Checkpoints are saved in browser IndexedDB; with the bridge, also in data/latest.json. Restore is explicit and never starts paid calls or raises request limits. The supplied recovery/turn-74.snapshot.json restores the provided v4 journal at its last committed turn; it contains no newly invented learning. Export retains the complete ledger without keys. Keep one active ORIGIN tab; the world engine runs in that browser, not autonomously on the server.

One HTML file, and one rendering dependency

The application is plain HTML and JavaScript. Three.js is loaded from the included optional local cache, then pinned public mirrors. Run node install-assets.cjs to populate the cache and embed it in ORIGIN.html. All humans and scenery are procedural; no external avatar is downloaded. Failure to load WebGL blocks model calls, rather than presenting fake activity.

Controls and privacy

Drag to orbit, scroll to approach, click a person to inspect. F toggles cinema; Space starts or pauses. Direct mode holds the key only in this tab. Bridge mode may use .env instead. The local server listens only on 127.0.0.1. This is a single-user development prototype, not a secure public service.

Why does the world stop with STALLED?

Observing the same scene no longer returns a generic new-memory reward. Three consecutive low-benefit outcomes, or four in a six-action window without learning, stop the run. Eating while nearly full and drinking while nearly hydrated count even when action names alternate. This spending guard does not replace decisions. Read the ledger, change the model context or enable planning, then explicitly resume. All choices stay with Jev; the runtime never swaps in a random activity just to make the world appear alive.

Your request budget.
