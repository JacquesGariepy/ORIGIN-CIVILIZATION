Connect their decisions.

Jev chooses for each individual. The engine runs the authorized activities in parallel and preserves their physical consequences.

Each person has a private context. A cohort of decisions is resolved together, not in network-arrival order. Social responses and learning require additional calls. The limits count attempts, not a guaranteed price. Existing activity progress is saved when paused.

Connect Jev and a planner

Launch the included server. Put TYPESAFE_API_KEY in .env, or paste the matching key in Connect TypeSafe. For cloud proposals, set PLANNER_API_KEY (an OpenRouter key) and PLANNER_MODEL. For your CLI, set AGY_ENABLED=1 after installing and authenticating agy. For any OpenAI-compatible API or a local LLM (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, vLLM), set LLM_BASE_URL and LLM_MODEL. For Claude Code or Codex, sign in to the CLI and set CLAUDE_ENABLED=1 or CODEX_ENABLED=1. The planner never executes an action: it proposes, Jev decides. See docs/LLM-PLANNERS.md.

Time, autonomy and failure

All lives share simulated time. Activities overlap and finish independently. Approved activities continue during new decisions for at most five simulated minutes; then time is held until the AI responds. A failure freezes the world without default behavior. Pausing retains ongoing activities. Clicking Review explicitly permits a new evaluation.

What is implemented, and what is not

Needs, private memories, bilateral social decisions, age-restricted affection, family links, simplified pregnancy/birth/growth, usable objects, occupancy locks, household storage, causal blueprints and finite-resource technology recipes are implemented. The final research recipe fits a small numeric predictor; it does not create a general AI.

The Earth atlas uses actual Natural Earth coastlines. A local terrain tile can be imported online; ecology and resources remain explicitly generated. Local rooms, furniture, funded contracts, household stores, power, water and personal works are persistent. Biology, emotion and material processes remain bounded game models. A connected planner may propose short dialogue only for people who have learned language. Photorealistic bodies, a complete planet simulation and unrestricted invention are not implemented. No subjective consciousness is asserted.

Life scale

Default: 365 simulated days per year and 280 days per pregnancy. For a visibly compressed generational experiment, change these values explicitly below. This changes the model of time, not a claim of realism.

All dialogue-like labels describe events; they are not a hidden thought transcript. Exact provider requests and responses are in Full ledger. The bundled isometric renderer works without a CDN. The optional Three.js renderer is MIT-licensed. Native TypeSafe API: https://api.typesafe.ai/v1/systemone.
