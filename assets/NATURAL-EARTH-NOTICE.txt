This export derives from the Natural Earth low-resolution geometry distributed in pyogrio's naturalearth_lowres fixture (177 input features). Geometries were dissolved to remove internal boundaries and simplified by 0.07 degrees. It is a land outline only, not a complete source of physical terrain or terrestrial resources.
